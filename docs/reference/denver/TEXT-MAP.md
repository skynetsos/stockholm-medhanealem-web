# Denver's page text: what we have, what we don't

For each page in [STRUCTURE.md](STRUCTURE.md): the source URL, what the page is about (described in
our own words), and whether the church has written its own text on that subject. **No text from
denvermedhanealem.org is copied into this repository or the CMS** (D-050). If the committee wants to
use any of it, the church first asks Denver's permission, and Denver sends the text.

Status key:
- **Ours (reference only)**: the church has written its own text on the subject (Amharic, with an English draft). Denver's page is at most a comparison.
- **Gap (candidate)**: we have nothing on this general Orthodox subject. Denver's text could be used once permission is confirmed, or the clergy write our own. Any teaching needs clergy approval either way.
- **Denver-specific**: about Denver's own parish (its people, times, history, projects, accounts). It can't be reused; Stockholm needs its own facts.
- **Form or function**: little text; the page is a form, an embed or a list built from data.

## Summary

- **Candidates once permission is confirmed (or for our clergy to write):** Tewahedo history, theology, holy scriptures, the five pillars of mystery, and five of the seven sacraments (confirmation, communion, ordination, penance, unction of the sick).
- **Ours already, Denver only for comparison:** our church and its history, Our Faith, christening, marriage, fitihat, offerings, the worship explanations.
- **Everything else** is Denver's own parish, or a form or list. It is not content to transfer.
